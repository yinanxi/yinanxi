## About Me

I am an undergraduate at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)**, pursuing a B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering and a second bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology, with graduation expected in June 2027.

At **MAIR Lab, HUST**, I work with [Prof. Zhiyuan Ma](https://ponymzy.github.io/) on diffusion-model alignment and lead the development of the OJ Tutor component of [EduNexus](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/edunexus/). I was a visiting student at [NC State ECE](https://ece.ncsu.edu/) in summer 2025 and a research intern at [United Imaging Healthcare](https://www.united-imaging.com/en/) in summer 2026.

## Research Interests

- **World models & generative modeling** — diffusion and flow models, reward guidance, alignment, and multimodal generation
- **Generative policies & physical AI** — VLA models, diffusion policies, embodied agents, spatial intelligence, and 3D perception
- **Generative modeling for biology** — protein and DNA generation, variable-length generation, and autonomous laboratories for drug discovery

## Selected Projects

- [**TMPO: Diffusion Alignment**](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/tmpo/) — trajectory-level reward-distribution matching for diverse and efficient diffusion alignment
- [**EduNexus: OJ Tutor**](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/edunexus/) — an evidence-grounded programming tutor within a university-wide multi-agent teaching platform
- [**Autonomous Ultrasound Scanning**](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/ultrasound/) — generative policies, VLA models, and simulation for autonomous ultrasound robotics
- [**3D Gaussian Splatting for Medical Image Registration**](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/gaussian-registration/) — sparse Gaussian deformation and adaptive density control
- [**LeakXplorer**](https://yinanxi.github.io/projects/leakxplorer/) — static analysis and language-model reasoning for privacy-leak detection
